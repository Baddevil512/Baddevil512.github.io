1. Executive Summary & Problem Breakdown

Jupiter DEX is undisputed king of Solana swap aggregators, processing over $1.5 Billion in daily volume. However, qualitative user research reveals four distinct user friction points:

  • MEV & Slippage Anxiety: Retail users frequently lose 1-3% on volatile memecoin trades to sandwich bots because MEV protection is tucked away in settings.
  • The Route Black Box: Jupiter's route list displays percentages in tiny collapsed menus, leaving users uncertain about how their funds are split across AMMs.
  • Form Fatigue: Traders executing repetitive moves (e.g. DCA into JUP or profit-taking to USDC) face repetitive manual dropdown picking.
  • Visual Noise: Existing Solana DEX interfaces are cluttered with low-contrast tables that degrade readability under high-stress volatile market events.

2. The Solution: Jupiter Horizon Terminal

Jupiter Horizon Terminal is an elite cyber-glassmorphism reimagining designed around three core pillars:

  • Zero-MEV Shield: Directly integrates Jito block bundles to guarantee private mempool transactions, eliminating sandwich attacks by default.
  • Interactive Dynamic SVG Multi-Hop Map: Visualizes live liquidity routing across Raydium, Orca, and Meteora with animated flow pulses so users immediately grasp the path of their tokens.
  • AI Smart Intent Bar: Enables conversational command parsing (e.g., "Swap 5 SOL to USDC with minimum slippage") to auto-fill tokens, balances, and safety parameters instantly.
